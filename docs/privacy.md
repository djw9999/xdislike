# Privacy Policy — Better X/Twitter

**Last updated:** 2026-09-04 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Who we are

Better X/Twitter is a Chrome extension for desktop X (twitter.com / x.com). Developer contact: djwkakarot@gmail.com.

## What the extension does

It runs locally in your browser to hide or rearrange UI on desktop X (promoted posts, Grok chrome, timeline grok posts, Discover more, media previews, view counts, Trends, optional Pin Following). It does not require signing into an X account.

## Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing data, X content, cookies, or credentials.

- **No X account tokens.** The extension does not read or store X authentication tokens.
- **No request sniffing.** It does not intercept or log network traffic to build a profile of you.
- **Local settings only.** Toggle state and an optional license key you paste are stored in Chrome extension storage on your device.

## Payments

A one-time license unlock is sold through Polar (polar.sh / org veedlure). Polar processes payment and issues a license key. That checkout is between you and Polar; we do not receive your card numbers. See Polar's own privacy policy for payment processing.

## Third parties

Chrome Web Store hosts the listing and installs. Polar handles license checkout if you buy. The extension does not embed analytics SDKs or ad trackers.

## Children's privacy

Not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

## Changes

We may update this page when the product's data practices change. The Last updated date will change.
