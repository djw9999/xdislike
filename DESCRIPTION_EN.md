# Better X/Twitter

**Hide promoted posts locally. Clean up desktop X in fewer clicks.**

Better X/Twitter is a lightweight Chrome extension for people who still use X on desktop but want a cleaner feed with less repetitive clicking. It runs locally in the browser, does not require an account, and stays focused on a few concrete cleanup tools instead of trying to replace the whole product.

## What changes after install

- **Hide promoted posts locally** while you browse.
- **Drag unwanted posts to the floating trash button** instead of opening menus one by one.
- **Open X in a mini window** for quick check-ins.
- **Collapse noisy community tabs** into one cleaner switcher.

## Why people install it

Desktop X gets noisy fast. Better X/Twitter is for people who still want to use the site, just with less clutter and less friction:

- remove obvious feed noise faster
- stay in control of what gets hidden
- keep the cleanup local to the browser

## Key features

### Hide promoted posts locally
Turn promoted-post cleanup on or off in the popup. This is UI-level cleanup inside your browser, not a heavy network blocker.

### Drag to hide
Drag a post into the floating trash button to remove it from your view. When possible, Better X/Twitter also triggers X's native "Not interested" feedback.

### Mini window
Open X in a compact floating window from the popup for short sessions and quick scans.
- Close it anytime with **Alt + X**.

### Merge community tabs
If your timeline has a long row of community/group tabs after **For you** and **Following**, Better X/Twitter can collapse them into one cleaner switcher while keeping the original tab behavior.

## How to use

1. Install the extension.
2. Open the popup and turn on the cleanup options you want.
3. Refresh X if it was already open.
4. Hide promoted posts, drag unwanted posts to the trash button, or use the mini window for quicker check-ins.
